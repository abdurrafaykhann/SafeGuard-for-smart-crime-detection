About SafeGuard

SafeGuard is an AI-powered platform designed to detect violent incidents and identify criminals in real time using YOLOv8 and facial recognition embeddings.

Key Benefits

• Real-time monitoring and instant alerts.
• Integrated criminal database.
• Civilian and law enforcement portals.
• Evidence management with timestamped logs.

How It Works

SafeGuard processes live video feeds, detects violence, and matches faces against a criminal database. Alerts and event logs are stored for further investigation and immediate action.
