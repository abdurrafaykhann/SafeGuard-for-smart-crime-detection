Privacy notice

SafeGuard is a research and demonstration system. This page describes exactly what it keeps.

What is stored

  • Alert snapshots: a single frame captured when an alert fires.
  • Watchlist entries: a name or label, the stated legal basis, a mathematical face signature and a tight crop of the face. Original photographs are never saved.
  • Audit log: who signed in, enrolled, searched or exported, with the time and IP address.

What is not stored

  • Continuous video recordings.
  • Uploaded analysis videos, which are deleted when processing finishes.
  • Face signatures of anyone who is not on the watchlist.

Retention

Alerts and their snapshots are deleted automatically after the configured retention period (30 days by default).

Your rights

Anyone enrolled can ask the administrator to remove their entry, which deletes the signature and the crop immediately.

Responsible AI & limitations

Automated surveillance can cause real harm when it is wrong. These are the limits you should know.

Violence detection is probabilistic

The score combines a trained detector with motion, contact and colour cues. Sports, dancing, crowded scenes, red clothing and poor lighting can all raise false alarms, and real incidents can be missed. The alert shows which cues contributed so an operator can judge quickly.

Face similarity is not identity

A match means two face signatures are numerically close. Accuracy varies with lighting, angle, image quality and across demographic groups. Treat every match as a lead to verify, never as proof.

Safeguards built in

  • Enrolment requires a recorded legal basis and explicit confirmation.
  • No automatic action is taken on any detection.
  • Operators can only acknowledge alerts. Only administrators can enrol or delete people.
  • Every sensitive action is audit-logged, and data expires automatically.

Not evaluated

This project has not been independently benchmarked for bias or accuracy. Do not deploy it for real enforcement decisions without that work.
