Capabilities

Everything an operations room needs, nothing it doesn't.

Multi-camera live wall

Webcams and RTSP/HTTP streams side by side, with resilient reconnects and per-camera analytics that never block each other.

Explainable violence detection

A YOLO model plus motion, contact and injury cues. Every alert shows why it fired, and sustained incidents escalate automatically.

Consent-based watchlist

Face matching runs only against people an administrator enrolled with a stated legal basis. Every lookup is audit-logged.

Instant notifications

Alerts stream to every open console in real time, and can fan out to a webhook, Telegram or email.

Recorded-video analysis

Drop in a clip and get an incident timeline, peak risk and evidence frames. The upload is deleted when processing ends.

Operational analytics

Trends by day and hour, hot-spot cameras and mean time-to-acknowledge, exportable to CSV.

Pipeline

From pixels to a decision in four steps.

Capture

One thread per source keeps only the newest frame, so latency never builds up.

Analyse

Detection runs once per camera on a throttled loop and is shared by every viewer.

Escalate

Cooldowns prevent alert storms; sustained incidents are promoted to critical.

Decide

An operator reviews the evidence, acknowledges and the action is recorded.

Responsible by design

Powerful tools need guardrails.

Human in the loop

The system recommends; people decide. No automatic action is ever taken on a match.

Consent & legal basis

No enrolment without a recorded basis and an explicit confirmation from the administrator.

Data minimisation & retention

Only face crops are stored, and alerts and snapshots are purged automatically after the retention period.

Honest limits

Similarity is a score, not proof. Read the limitations before relying on any result.
