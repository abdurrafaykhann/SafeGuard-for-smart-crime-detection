What you are looking at

This is a recording of the real detectors (the same ones a live camera uses, at 8 frames per second) run over five short, licence-free stock clips. Nothing is computed in your browser. We chose clips that show the system working; it is not perfect. On other footage it can miss close-up knives, mistake a phone for a pistol, or raise false alarms in low light. The measured results, including the failures, are in the project's evaluation report.

A weapon box appears once the detector has confirmed a sighting across several frames (the rule that raises an alert) and is then tracked. Footage: Pexels (free licence). Want the real thing live? Try the public fraud check or ask for a walkthrough.
