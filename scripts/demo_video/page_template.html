About this demo

All footage is free-licence stock (Pexels) and shows staged or prop scenes. Faces are blurred and every record is fictional. The clips were chosen to show the software working: on a wider trial of 23 stock clips the violence classifier also raised false alarms on calm street footage, which is why the closing card quotes the measured validation numbers (79% recall, 81% precision on 400 held-out clips) rather than these examples.
